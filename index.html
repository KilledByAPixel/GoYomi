    The rules in one minute

    Players take turns placing a stone on an empty intersection. Stones never move.

    Liberties are the empty points right next to a group (up, down, left, right). Stones touching in a line share liberties.

    Capture: fill a group's last liberty and it's removed. A group with one liberty left is in atari.

    No suicide: you can't play where your stone would have no liberties, unless that move captures.

    Ko: if you just captured one stone, your opponent can't immediately recapture in a way that repeats the board. They must play elsewhere first.

    End: when both players pass in a row. Your score is your stones plus the empty points only you surround. White gets komi for moving second.

    Life and death

    A group is safe forever if it has two separate eyes (enclosed empty points). The opponent can't fill both, because playing into either one alone would be suicide.

    A group with only one eye, or none, can eventually be surrounded and captured.

    Turn on Territory to see which groups the coach thinks are dead. They get a small square.

    Tips for 9×9
    • Start in the centre (E5) or on the 3-3 / 3-4 points (C3, G7…). First-line moves are small early on.
    • Stay connected. On a small board, cut-apart groups die fast.
    • Before each move, look for groups in atari (yours and theirs).
    • Don't chase stones you can't capture. Build territory while you attack.
    • Capturing is nice, but the winner is whoever surrounds more area.
    • Unsure? Hover a point: the preview shows captures, ataris and your stone's liberties.
